Read it in three acts

  • The Reader proposes. successor/agent/reader.py gives Qwen qwen4-max the notice text and two tools, follow_link and submit_proposal. It never receives a CMC price and has no verdict field to fill.
  • The Inspector checks. successor/verify/ keeps a field only if its quote is verbatim (C3), matches the successor and contract to CMC's rows (C2), reads both markets (C5), confirms the ratio within ±5% (C4) and guards price integrity (C6). An ambiguous successor sends one refute reason back for one repair turn.
  • The rules decide. successor/verify/verdict.py is a pure first-match table, R3 to R10. SWAP needs a verified successor and a price-confirmed ratio.
